TorrStream Lite

Privacy Policy

Effective November 9, 2026

TorrStream Lite is a torrent streaming app that runs entirely on your computer. It has no account and no analytics. The developer runs no servers and never receives any data about you or what you watch.

What the app connects to

  • The trackers and peers of the torrents you add, and the BitTorrent DHT network, to find peers. As with any torrent app, they see your IP address and which torrent you download. Uploading to other peers is turned off.
  • The address of a torrent you add by its URL, to download the .torrent file.

The app doesn't check for updates and connects to nothing else. Links in its Settings open in your browser.

What it keeps

  • The video stays in memory (RAM) only, and is released when you stop the torrent or quit the app. Nothing you watch is written to disk, and there's no history or library.
  • Watched positions from Lampa are kept in memory until the torrent is stopped.
  • The Mac app saves its own settings on your Mac: whether other devices are allowed, the port, the appearance, and the window's size and position.

Other devices on your network

Allow other devices is off by default. While it's on, anything on your home network can add, play and stop torrents without a password, including web pages open on its devices. It listens only on your home network's private addresses.

Changes

If what the app connects to or keeps changes, this page will say so, with a new effective date.
