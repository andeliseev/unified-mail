Unified Mail

A small, fast desktop email client for Gmail, with one inbox for all your accounts. For Windows and macOS.

  • All your Gmail accounts in one inbox, or one account at a time
  • New mail appears within seconds, with a notification and the unread count on the app icon
  • Read, archive, delete and mark as unread, with keyboard shortcuts
  • Runs entirely on your computer and talks only to Google, plus GitHub for updates
  • Updates itself with one click

Unified Mail is free and open source (MIT license). Download the latest version or see the source code on GitHub. If it's useful to you, you can support its development.

Unified Mail uses Google sign-in to access your Gmail. Your email never leaves your computer except to and from Google. Read the Privacy Policy for details.
