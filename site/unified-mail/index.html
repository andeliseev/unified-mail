Unified Mail

A small, fast desktop email client for Gmail, with one inbox for all your accounts. For Windows and macOS.

  • All your Gmail accounts in one inbox, or one account at a time
  • New mail appears within seconds
  • Read, archive and delete, with keyboard shortcuts
  • Runs entirely on your computer and talks only to Google

Unified Mail uses Google sign-in to access your Gmail. Your email never leaves your computer except to and from Google. Read the Privacy Policy for details.
