Unified Mail

Privacy Policy

Effective November 4, 2026

Unified Mail is a desktop email program for Gmail that runs entirely on your computer. It connects only to Google, and to GitHub to check for updates. The developer runs no servers and never receives your email or any other data about you.

What the app accesses

When you add an account, you sign in with Google and allow Unified Mail to access your Gmail account (permission https://mail.google.com/). The app uses this access only to:

  • show the messages in your Inbox, All Mail and Trash: sender, recipients, subject, date, preview and content;
  • mark a message as read when you open it, and as read or unread when you ask it to;
  • archive a message, move it to Trash, or move it back out (Undo, Move to Inbox), when you ask it to;
  • get notified when your Inbox changes (over IMAP), so new mail appears right away;
  • read your email address, to label the account in the app.

The app does not send email, permanently delete email, or change your Gmail settings. Google requires the full Gmail permission for IMAP notifications, which is why its consent screen lists more than the app uses.

Where your data goes

All requests go directly from your computer to Google (accounts.google.com, oauth2.googleapis.com, gmail.googleapis.com, imap.gmail.com). Nothing is sent to the developer or to any other server. The app has no analytics, telemetry, advertising or crash reporting.

To check for updates, the app downloads a small file from GitHub (github.com), where it is published: when it starts, every few hours, and when you open Settings. Like any download, this shows GitHub your IP address, but nothing about your accounts or mail. An update is only installed when you click Update in Settings, and only if it is signed by the developer.

Emails can contain images stored on other websites. Like most email programs, Unified Mail loads them when you open a message, so the website hosting an image can see your IP address and that the image was loaded. Links you click open in your web browser.

What is stored on your computer

  • Sign-in tokens: in your operating system's secure storage (Windows Credential Manager or macOS Keychain).
  • Your list of accounts and a copy of the Inbox, All Mail and Trash lists (sender, subject, preview, date, read state), in the app's data folder, so the app can open instantly.
  • Message content is not saved. It is downloaded from Google each time you open a message.
  • Notifications about new mail show its sender and subject. Windows and macOS keep recent notifications in their notification center until you clear them.

How data is shared

It isn't. Your data is not sold, shared with anyone, used for advertising, used to train AI or machine-learning models, or read by people.

Unified Mail's use and transfer to any other app of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Your choices and deleting your data

  • Remove an account in the app's Settings. This revokes the app's access at Google and deletes that account's sign-in token and cached message lists from your computer.
  • You can revoke access at any time in your Google Account at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
  • To remove everything, uninstall the app and delete its data folder: %APPDATA%\io.unifiedmail.desktop on Windows, or ~/Library/Application Support/io.unifiedmail.desktop on macOS.

Children

Unified Mail is not directed at children under 13.
